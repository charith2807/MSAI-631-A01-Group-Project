## How an applicant tracking system reads a résumé
An applicant tracking system (ATS) extracts the raw text from your file, finds sections by matching headings against standard names, maps the content to fields such as employer, title, dates, and skills, and lets recruiters search and filter. Most failures happen at the parsing step. There is no secret universal ATS score, so treat any score as a heuristic.

## Layout rules the checker applies
Use a single column with a simple reading order. Avoid tables and text boxes for core content because parsers may read them out of order or skip them. Keep your contact details in the body of the document rather than only in the header or footer, which some parsers ignore. Submit text-based PDF or DOCX files, never a scanned image.

## Standard headings
Use plain section names such as Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, and Certifications. Creative titles such as "My Journey" or "Where I've Been" can cause a section to be missed.

## Dates, bullets, and length
Use one consistent date format throughout, for example "Jan 2022 – Mar 2024". Start bullets with action verbs and include numbers or concrete results where honest. Aim for one page early in your career and two pages at most.

## Keywords
Matching is largely literal. If the job says "TypeScript", "JavaScript" does not satisfy it. Use the exact term from the posting for skills you genuinely have, spell out acronyms once, and do not repeat keywords artificially.
