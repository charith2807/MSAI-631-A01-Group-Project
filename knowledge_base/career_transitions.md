## Starting a transition
Clarify why you want to change and what you want from the next role. List the skills, interests, and values you want to carry over, and research target roles through job postings and conversations with people already in them.

## Transferable skills
Skills such as communication, project management, analysis, customer focus, and leadership carry across fields. Translate your past experience into the language of the new field: instead of the old job title, describe the problem you solved and the outcome. Always stay truthful and tie each claim to something you actually did.

## Closing skill gaps
Compare your current skills to the target role, identify the critical gaps, and build a plan: a focused course, a portfolio project, volunteer or freelance work, or a stretch assignment at your current employer. A bridging project is often the strongest evidence for hiring managers.

## Positioning your story
Prepare a short, honest narrative: where you came from, what drew you to the new field, what you have done to prepare, and why you are a strong fit. Update your résumé summary and LinkedIn headline to match the target role.

## Practical paths
Consider a stepping-stone role that is adjacent to your target, an internal move within your company, a part-time or contract role, or a structured program such as a bootcamp or graduate course. Expect the transition to take months, and keep networking throughout.
